How it is built

The wall is the artwork repeated as the background, with an iris and a pupil drawn in SVG over each of its eyes, clipped to that eye's white along a smooth outline, so the iris slides under the lids. Every iris is one shared drawing (<use>) that takes its own colour from a custom property; the lids, the swell and the roll are keyframes, and each eye waits by its distance from the click, so an effect spreads as a wave. Reactive CSS adds the interaction, with no application JavaScript:

  • One mouse() reading serves every eye: each knows its own place on the screen and turns by its offset over max(distance, 25vmin), worked out once in registered properties.
  • A click keeps where it landed, from event(client-x) and event(client-y), and counts; the count's parity picks one of two identical animations, so a finished blink plays again on the next click.
  • Colour steps through six iris colours in a state(); a style query sets each, and a registered <color> eases it in.
  • The buttons pick the effect with :on(click), and :when() marks the one chosen. Only the tiles the screen shows are drawn: size container queries hide the rest.
